Video Content Finalization

Locked content plan for the 10‑minute project video, point‑weighted to the rubric and tied to existing assets.

Why this page exists. An earlier 13‑slide deck and a rubric mapping already exist in 07_video_rubric_plan.html and 08_video_slides.html. This page is the final lock‑in: it commits to a 12‑slide structure, allocates seconds in proportion to rubric points, names the headline number for every slide, and lists the open content decisions to confirm before recording.

Top‑line decisions

  1. Length: 10‑minute hard cap. Plan budgets 600 seconds and leaves ~30s of slack for trimming during recording.
  2. Slide count: 12 slides, avg ~50s per slide. Tighter than the existing 13‑slide deck so the closing rubric items get the time they are worth in points.
  3. Time allocation by rubric points. The previous plan over‑weighted Results (3:30) and under‑weighted Difficulty (0:50). Difficulty is worth 20 points — it gets a real slide here.
  4. Story arc. Question → Setup → Four results → Class & Difficulty → Close. The phase+geometry diagnostic is the headline (it is the most surprising and best‑evidenced finding); the visual‑axis ranking is the warm‑up.
  5. Headline takeaway. "Visual diversity matters — but obstacle‑aware reaching is bottlenecked by phase and geometry inference, not appearance." This is the thesis the closing slide must land.
