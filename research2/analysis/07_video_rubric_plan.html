Equations and Formulations to Show

The rubric explicitly asks for key equations/formulations. These are enough for the video without making it overly theoretical.

NameFormulaPurpose
Behavior cloning objectivemin_theta sum_t || pi_theta(o_t, q_t) - a*_t ||_2^2Shows the supervised learning problem: match expert actions from observations and robot state.
Closed-loop rollouts_{t+1} = f(s_t, pi_theta(o_t, q_t))Shows why small action errors compound after the policy controls the robot.
Precision successsuccess@epsilon = 1[min_t ||p_ee,t - p_target||_2 <= epsilon]Defines S@0.5cm, S@1cm, S@2cm, and S@5cm from closest distance reached.
Final stabilityfinal_distance = ||p_ee,T - p_target||_2Separates reaching near the target from staying near the target at the end of rollout.

Class Concepts to Name Explicitly

Class conceptProject connection
Behavior cloningThe policy is trained by supervised imitation of scripted expert actions.
Covariate shiftClosed-loop errors move the robot into states different from demonstrations.
Closed-loop evaluationThe project evaluates rollouts, not just train/validation loss.
GeneralizationID/OOD splits isolate color, spatial, camera, and lighting shifts.
Human-robot interactionA human data designer decides what demonstrations and diversity to collect so the robot behaves robustly.

Suggested Closing Slide

Takeaway. Visual diversity matters, but obstacle-aware robot imitation is not solved by collecting more varied images alone. The hardest failures come from spatial, phase, and geometry inference under closed-loop control. Precision metrics reveal this, and the phase+geometry diagnostic shows what information the visual policy was missing.
